# Privacy Policy for Kingdom Grid

**Last updated:** October 15, 2026

Kingdom Grid ("we", "our", or "us") is a strategy game for Android. This
policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, and the
choices available to you.

## Information the app handles

### Local game data

Campaign progress, board state, action history, settings, achievements, and
local profile statistics are stored on your device using Android Room and
DataStore. This data is not uploaded unless you choose a network-backed
feature that synchronizes it.

### Account and cloud-play data

If you sign in with Firebase Authentication (email/password, Google, or
anonymous guest access), Firebase creates an account identifier. Depending on
the sign-in method and the profile information you provide, the service may
also process your display name, email address, and profile photo URL.

Online multiplayer and cloud synchronization can store game identifiers,
match state, ordered player identifiers, action history, scores, friends, and
profile or achievement data in Firebase Firestore or Realtime Database.
These values are used to provide matchmaking, invitations, synchronization,
replays, and account features. The app does not use this data for advertising.

### Notifications

If notifications are enabled, Firebase Cloud Messaging processes a device
registration token and notification delivery information. The app stores the
token with the signed-in profile so it can deliver match and invitation
notifications. You can disable notifications in Android system settings.

### Analytics and diagnostics

Firebase Analytics may process app-instance, device, operating-system, and
usage information, such as selected game mode, match duration, scores,
outcomes, settings, and achievement events. Kingdom Grid's analytics events
do not include account IDs, game IDs, friend IDs, invitation codes, or FCM
tokens. Firebase may process technical diagnostics needed to operate the
service.

### Nearby multiplayer

The optional Nearby multiplayer mode uses Google Play services Nearby
Connections and Android Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and location-related permissions
where required by the Android version. Discovery and game messages are
exchanged between nearby devices for the session. The app does not use
Nearby data for advertising or maintain a separate location history.

### More Games catalog

The optional More Games screen can request a public catalog and icon URLs over
HTTPS from GitHub-hosted resources. Those requests can expose normal network
metadata, such as your IP address and user agent, to the hosting provider.
The app does not send your Kingdom Grid account ID with those requests. You
can use the core game without refreshing this catalog.

## Service providers and sharing

We use Google Firebase and Google Play services for authentication, cloud
storage, analytics, messaging, Google sign-in, and Nearby Connections when
you enable the relevant features. Public catalog and icon requests go to the
configured HTTPS hosting providers. We do not sell personal information and
do not use it for third-party advertising.

## Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall
the app. Cloud data is retained while needed to provide your account and
online features. You may stop using cloud features, sign out, or request
account and associated cloud-data deletion by contacting us. We will verify
the request and delete or de-identify data that we control, subject to
legitimate security, fraud-prevention, and legal retention requirements.

## Security

We use the security controls provided by Android, Firebase, and Google Play
services, and restrict cloud access through the app's authentication and
database rules. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed
to be completely secure.

## Children's privacy

Kingdom Grid is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly
collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you
believe a child has provided personal information so that we can review and
delete it.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The
updated date above will identify the latest version.
